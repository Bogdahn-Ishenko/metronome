# Online Metronome

A free browser metronome for music practice. Set the tempo and meter, work on your rhythm, and bookmark your preferred settings.

## Features

- Tempo from 20 to 300 BPM, plus preset tempo markings.
- Meters with 2 to 12 beats and note values of 2, 4, 8, or 16.
- Groups of three for compound meters such as 6/8, 9/8, and 12/8.
- Half-note, triplet, and quarter-note subdivisions.
- Tap tempo to estimate a beat from repeated taps.
- Adjustable volume, mute, and beat accents.
- Light, dark, and automatic themes.
- Russian, English, Spanish, French, and German interfaces.
- Settings persist between visits. The current settings are included in the page URL, so presets can be bookmarked or shared.
- Focused pages for guitar practice (`/en/metronome-for-guitar/`), 6/8 meter (`/en/6-8-metronome/`), and tap tempo (`/en/tap-tempo/`).

## How to use it

1. Set the tempo with the controls or tap along with the Tap button.
2. Choose a meter, subdivisions, and accents.
3. Press Start to begin.
4. Bookmark or share the page URL to keep the current settings.

Press `Space` to start or stop. `Up Arrow` increases tempo by 1 BPM, and `Down Arrow` decreases it by 1 BPM; hold `Shift` for 10 BPM steps. Press `T` to tap the tempo.
